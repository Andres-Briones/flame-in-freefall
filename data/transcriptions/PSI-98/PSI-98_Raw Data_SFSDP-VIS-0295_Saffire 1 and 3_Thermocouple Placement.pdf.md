Additional Information:

**R.SFSD_S.0003** - SFS Demo Flame Spread Experiment Sample Count
The SFS Demo Structural Subsystem shall have a single sample for use in the Flame Spread experiment sample burn.

Success Criteria: Verification shall be considered successful when the inspection prior to product delivery to AI&T shows that 1 sample is prepared for use in the Flame Spread experiment burn.

Verification Approach: SFS Demo Flame Spread Experiment Sample Count shall be verified by Inspection. An inspection shall be performed of the loaded Flame Spread Sample holder.

---

**R.SFSD_S.0004** - SFS Demo Flame Spread Sample Size
The SFS Demo Structural Subsystem shall have a Flame Spread Sample having width of 12.0 in (300 mm) and length of 31.5 in (800 mm), with a goal width of 19.7 in (500 mm) and goal length of 39.4 in (1000 mm).

Success Criteria: Verification shall be considered successful when the inspection prior to product delivery to AI&T shows that the SFS Demo Structural Subsystem has a Flame Spread Sample having width of 12.0 in (300 mm), length of 31.5 in (800 mm), with a goal width of 19.7 in (500 mm) and goal length of 39.4 in (1000 mm).

Verification Approach: SFS Demo Flame Spread Sample Size shall be verified by Inspection for Saffire-I and Saffire-III. An inspection shall be performed of the flight hardware per the as-built assembly drawing 120102MFA310.

---

**R.SFSD_S.00043** - SFS Demo Flame and Fuel Surface Temperature Measurement (Flame Spread Sample)
The SFS Demo Structural Subsystem shall measure the temperature of the Flame Spread Sample flame zone at 6 locations as specified in Table 3.7.4-7 and depicted in Figure 3.7.4-1.

Success Criteria: Verification shall be considered successful when the inspection shows that the temperature measurement sensors are installed at 6 locations on the Flame Spread Sample as specified in Table 3.7.4-7 and depicted in Figure 3.7.4-1.

Verification Approach: SFS Demo Flame and Fuel Surface Temperature Measurement (Flame Spread Sample) shall be verified by Inspection. An inspection shall be performed of the flight hardware per the as-built assembly drawings 120102MFA000 and 120102MFA310.
